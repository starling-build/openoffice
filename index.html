STARLING OFFICE / OPEN SOURCE

Your Office.
Your device.

  • No cloud uploads

Writer, Slides, and Sheets. A free, open source office suite in your browser or as a native app.

Open, edit, and save DOCX, PPTX, and XLSX locally. In both versions, your documents stay on your device, without being uploaded to our servers.
